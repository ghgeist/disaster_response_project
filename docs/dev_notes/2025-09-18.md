### **2025-09-18: Hierarchy Constraints Finalized — Zero Violations, Minimal F1 Impact**

**🎯 Major Achievement**: Completed and validated the hierarchy post-processor. Achieved zero parent≥child violations with macro/weighted F1 impact within target, while maintaining Safety Recall on critical labels. Integrated evaluation, artifact persistence, and configuration for reproducibility.

## **Critical Problem Solved**

Previously, predictions violated label hierarchy (children > parent; child=1 without parent=1), undermining interpretability and safety. Rare-class safety performance was a concern. Today’s work enforces consistent parent–child relationships and validates that safety remains strong without degrading overall performance beyond acceptable limits.

## **Solution Implemented: Hierarchy Post-Processing**

### **1. Taxonomy & Config**
- Canonical `TAXONOMY`, `CRITICAL_LABELS`, and `EXCLUDE_FROM_CONSTRAINTS` added to `src/disasterproject/utils/config.py`.
- Special handling: `related` group uses decision-only forcing (no probability clamping). `child_alone` excluded due to 0 positives.
- Config toggle: `HIERARCHY_CRITICAL_THRESHOLD_REDUCTION = 0.0` (optimized from earlier -0.10 assumption).

### **2. Core Algorithm**
- Monotonic probabilities for true parent groups (aid/infrastructure/weather): parent ≥ max(children), children ≤ parent.
- Decision-level rule: if any child=1 ⇒ force parent=1.
- Exceptions respected for excluded labels and the `related` group (decision-only).

### **3. Evaluation, Metrics, and Artifacts**
- Normalized “violations per 1k” metric to be per parent→child edge.
- Persisted exact thresholds used for decisions:
  - Production eval: `model/thresholds_used_hierarchy.json`
  - Experimental runs: `experiments/hierarchy_evaluation/thresholds_used_hierarchy_<timestamp>.json`
- Results saved under `experiments/optimized_hierarchy_final/` (CSV/JSON) and appended to `model/performance_metrics.csv`.
