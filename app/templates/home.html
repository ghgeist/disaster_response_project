How It Works

Data Cleaning

Merge messages and categories, remove ambiguities and duplicates, and load into a database for a solid training set.

Feature Extraction (TF-IDF)

Custom tokenizer normalizes text and TF-IDF emphasizes critical words while downweighting common terms.

Multi-Label Model

A MultiOutput Random Forest tags each message with all relevant categories across 36 disaster needs.
